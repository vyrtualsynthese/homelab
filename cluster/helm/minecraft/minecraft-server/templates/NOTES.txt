The Minecraft server is deliberately not running: the Deployment renders with
replicas: 0 until the anti-cheat, anti-grief and backup work lands.

The world claim {{ include "app.dataClaimName" . }} does not bind until a pod uses
it, so a first start is also when the volume is created. Until then the
PersistentVolumeClaim is Pending and ArgoCD shows this app as Progressing — that
is expected while replicas is 0, not drift. The world is new; the pre-Longhorn
world is gone.

To start it, set replicaCount to 1 in values.yaml and let the chart sync. Watch
the first boot: the startup probe gives it ten minutes, and downloading the pinned
plugins needs egress to github.com and download.luckperms.net.

The Service is only reachable through the mc entryPoint. Port 25565 at the router
must stay closed until the security work is accepted.
